The Logbook Myth: What Really Happens at Borders

Customs NEVER keeps your original vehicle logbook. Officials only inspect the original, verify the chassis/engine numbers against your car, stamp your TIP or CPD, and hand the logbook right back to you. Carry 10 photocopies in a dedicated folder.
